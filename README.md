# FIRE Apps

Four small, focused iOS apps for people working toward **F**inancial
**I**ndependence, **R**etire **E**arly — built by one person who needed them,
shared with everyone who does too.

Each app owns one letter of F·I·R·E:

| Letter | App | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F** | **FIRE Finance** | Personal finance | Planned |
| **I** | **FIRE Mileage** | Vehicle mileage tracking + IRS standard-mileage deduction | On the App Store |
| **R** | **FIRE Realty** | Rental property / real-estate tracking | In development |
| **E** | **FIRE Biz** | Small-business expense tracking (Schedule C ready) | TestFlight |

All four are local-first SwiftUI apps — your data lives on your device (with
optional private iCloud sync), no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
